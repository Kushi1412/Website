The Problem

Pilots everywhere, production nowhere

The typical pattern: an ambitious AI pilot on forecasting or analytics, a proof-of-concept that impresses in a demo, and then — nothing. No production deployment, no measurable savings, and a finance team more sceptical than before.

The cause is almost always the same: starting with the most ambitious use case instead of the most automatable one. High-judgement processes are the hardest to automate and the riskiest to get wrong. Meanwhile, thousands of hours sit in well-understood, rule-heavy work that nobody finds exciting enough to fix.

And underneath it all: data that isn't ready, controls that aren't defined, and no answer to the question every auditor and regulator will ask — who is accountable when the machine is wrong?

What We Do

Automation with governance built in

We identify the processes where automation pays back fastest, design the target workflow, select pragmatic tooling, and deploy it with the control framework your auditors will expect. Security is not a chapter at the end — data residency, access control, audit trails and human-in-the-loop checkpoints are part of the design from day one.

Because we come from group finance and integration work — not from a software vendor — we have no tool to sell you. We recommend what fits your landscape, and we stay accountable for whether it works in month-end reality, not just in the demo.

Where We Typically Start

High volume, clear rules, measurable hours

01 —

Reconciliations

Bank, intercompany and balance-sheet reconciliations — matching logic is rule-based, volume is high, and exceptions are where human judgement actually belongs.

02 —

Entity Reporting

Recurring statutory and group reporting across entities — same structure every month, painful to compile manually, ideal for automated assembly with human review.

03 —

Compliance Checks

Deadline monitoring, filing-completeness checks and policy screening across jurisdictions — high stakes, fully rule-driven, perfect for machines with an audit trail.

Once these run reliably, the organisation has the data discipline, the controls and the confidence to take on AI-assisted forecasting, anomaly detection and analytics — with a track record behind it, not guesswork.
