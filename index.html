Case Study
Danish Manufacturing SME · Reporting Platform

A plug-and-play reporting platform, built to management's own criteria

No platform to track the year, no credible full-year forecast — and a clear ask for one automated view of daily operations. We built it around management's own criteria.

What we did

We reviewed the company's existing material and systems, designed the operating platform around management's own criteria for what it had to pull, and — through close dialogue with the leadership team — concluded on a single financial platform for daily, monthly, quarterly and yearly reporting. It consolidates data automatically from the existing sources into one source of truth: year-to-date actuals against forecast, KPI dashboards, variance and deviation tracking, cash-flow and forecast modelling, and drill-down by product, seller and period — including month-versus-same-month-last-year comparatives and a clear view of individual sellers' performance.

Outcome

Management gained a daily, self-updating view of where the business stood and where the year was heading, month-end reporting got faster, and decisions that used to wait on manual spreadsheets now rest on one trusted platform tailored to how the company actually runs.

Case Study
Danish Public-Sector Tax Authority · Forecasting & Reporting

Forecasting the right control strategy under a fraud-case backlog

A major dividend-tax fraud case, a growing backlog, and interest accruing on every case left open past six months. The authority needed the optimal control strategy — so we modelled it.

What we did

We built a forecasting model framed as an optimisation problem with three unknowns — the case backlog (sagsbunker), the number of cases by size (small, medium, large) and case-handling time — made harder by the six-month interest rule, which created pressure to prioritise larger cases and clear them before the deadline. The model determined the optimal control strategy and became the basis for how the controls strategy was set in that area. Separately, we developed a fully automated Power BI management-reporting platform built on SAP data extracts, with monthly performance logging and written explanations of deviations, used for management reporting across the ministry.

Outcome

The authority had an evidence-based control strategy it could defend and adjust as the backlog moved, and a standing, automated reporting platform that gave management a monthly, explained view of performance without manual assembly.
