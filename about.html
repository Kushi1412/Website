Our Approach

We start in finance. We don't stop there.

Finance transformation fails when it's scoped as a pure finance initiative. Every operating model change requires people outside finance to change how they work — and people change how they work when they own the change, not when they're consulted about it.

We work across finance, legal, tax, HR and technology because that's how reality works. Leading finance integration hand-in-hand with the IMO taught us that cross-functional ownership determines whether a transformation lands or stalls — so we design ownership before we design processes.

And we stay flexible on role: strategic advisor at board level on some engagements, hands-on in the engine room on others. In the mid-market and PE segment, that range is rare — and it's exactly what the work needs.

What you can hold us to:

  • Honest scoping — if the answer is "don't do this transformation", that's the advice you'll get
  • Cross-functional by design — ownership maps and decision rights before process diagrams
  • Deliverables sized for action — mid-market organisations need to move this quarter, not feed a programme office
  • Accountability past the handover — through our shared services center, we live with the operational consequences of our own designs
