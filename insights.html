Case Studies

Lessons from past engagements

Anonymised to protect client confidentiality — every lesson here was earned on real work, not invented for marketing.

Global Healthcare Group · M&A

Integrating an acquisition's finance function across 12 entities

Following a multi-billion acquisition, the acquired group's entities had to report into group consolidation within two quarters — different chart of accounts, different calendar, different controls.

What we did

Led the finance integration workstream in close coordination with the IMO: Day-1 readiness, reporting alignment, entity governance integration, and a strict separation of the integration backlog from transformation ambitions.

Outcome

All entities reporting to group standards on deadline; transformation items handed over as a scoped, costed programme rather than scope creep.

International Engineering Group · Transformation

Cross-functional governance for a group finance transformation

A finance transformation kept stalling at the same point: decisions that needed legal, HR and IT were parked because no forum owned them.

What we did

Redesigned the programme governance around a cross-functional ownership map: every workstream co-owned by finance and the function it affected, with decision rights agreed before design work resumed.

Outcome

Blocked decisions cleared within two governance cycles; the operating model went live with functions defending the design as their own — because it was.

PE-Backed Mid-Market Group · Entity Governance

Exit-readiness across a grown-not-designed structure

Ahead of a planned exit, a PE-backed group faced a structure of 20+ entities accumulated through acquisitions — including dormant companies with filing gaps.

What we did

Full entity portfolio review: purpose, cost and risk per entity; wind-down of surplus entities; remediation of statutory gaps; and a standing exit-readiness record replacing the last-minute data room scramble.

Outcome

A simplified, documented structure that survived buyer due diligence without a single entity-level finding.
